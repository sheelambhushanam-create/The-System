## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, biometric and wellness foundation engineered to adapt across three expressive behavioral modes while maintaining a singular, uncompromising architectural spine:

1. **Universal Clean (Default):** Engineered minimalism for data-driven athletes and everyday health tracking. Grounded, scientific, and hyper-legible with restrained technological authority.
2. **Women-Centric Wellness:** A biological, rhythmic approach prioritizing hormonal cycles, maternal vitality, and restorative states. Soft, nurturing, and refined without sacrificing quantitative clarity.
3. **Hunter RPG / Solo Leveling:** Gamified bio-optimization translating physiological adaptation into quest mechanics, stat progression, and hunter rank indicators. Electric, commanding, and tactilely futuristic.

Across all modes, the interface rejects visual clutter in favor of absolute functional clarity, typographic discipline, and sensory responsiveness. Transitions between modes retain strict spatial alignment, navigational constancy, and accessible contrast ratios.

## Colors

The design system operates on an intrinsically dark baseline to reduce retinal strain during pre-dawn workouts, recovery cycles, and deep sleep transitions. Themes share a shared tokenized semantic palette mapped to distinct behavioral palettes:

### Universal Clean (Engineered Kinetic)
- **Canvas / Surface:** Deep Slate (`#0b0f19`), Card Layer (`#131b2e`), Surface High (`#1e293b`)
- **Primary:** Electric Cyan (`#06b6d4`) — active telemetry, energetic states
- **Secondary:** Kinetic Emerald (`#10b981`) — goal completion, biometric homeostasis
- **Text:** High-contrast White (`#f8fafc`), Muted Slate (`#94a3b8`)

### Women-Centric Wellness (Hormonal & Lunar Rhythms)
- **Canvas / Surface:** Warm Basalt (`#141216`), Card Layer (`#1e1a22`), Surface High (`#2a2430`)
- **Primary:** Rose Quartz (`#f472b6`) — luteal/follicular cycle focus, heart rate reserve
- **Secondary:** Terracotta & Sage (`#fb923c`, `#34d399`) — metabolic warmth and restorative recovery
- **Accent:** Dream Lavender (`#c084fc`) — sleep architecture and maternal mindfulness
- **Text:** Silk White (`#fdf4f5`), Dusty Mauve (`#a89fa8`)

### Hunter RPG (Bio-Awakening HUD)
- **Canvas / Surface:** Obsidian Void (`#05080f`), HUD Panel Layer (`#09101d`), Surface High (`#111d33`)
- **Primary:** Holographic Plasma Cyan (`#00e5ff`) — mana reserves, system announcements, current quest
- **Secondary:** Rank Gold / Overdrive Crimson (`#fbbf24`, `#ff0055`) — level milestones, threshold penalties
- **Border / Accents:** Neon Cyan Outlines (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`) with high-energy edge lighting
- **Text:** Polar Luminescence (`#f0fdff`), Shadow Steel (`#64748b`)

## Typography

Typography balances precision telemetry against human emotional connection:

- **Headlines & Structural Titles (Space Grotesk):** Provides technological tension, sharp geometry, and modern performance branding. For Hunter RPG mode, key display counters switch character tracking to strict tabular spacing.
- **Continuous Body & Narrative (Inter):** High-density readability for coaching logs, maternal nutritional insights, and exercise mechanics. Zero glyph ambiguity across multi-line content.
- **Biometric Readouts, Data & Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced numeric alignment guarantees that live stats (heart rate, wattage, quest timers, cycle countdowns) update seamlessly without horizontal layout jitter.

Headings adopt strict sentence casing to maintain accessibility and quiet authority, reserving all-caps styling exclusively for micro-labels (`label-badge`) and Hunter mode status rankings.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an 8pt base grid with a fluid 4-column structure on mobile devices, reflowing into an 8-column layout on tablets and a 12-column fixed container (maximum 1280px) on desktop viewports.

### Fluid Adaptation
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-stream vertical telemetry. 16px screen edge margins (`margin`) allow maximum finger surface scanning. Cards span full width.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** Split-column configuration pairing live metrics with active logs or maternal progress modules.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Centralized dashboard shell flanked by secondary telemetry docks and persistent system status navigation.

Component interior padding defaults strictly to `space-md` for standard telemetry blocks, contracting to `space-sm` for dense metric rows and expanding to `space-lg` for cycle visualizers and Hunter quest completion modals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tiered background luminance and theme-specific lighting rather than heavy generic drop shadows:

- **Universal Clean:** Built on pure tonal layers. Base canvas `#0b0f19` supports Level 1 cards (`#131b2e`), with actionable components set on Level 2 surfaces (`#1e293b`). Subtle 1px ghost borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) separate containers cleanly without visual noise.
- **Women-Centric Wellness:** Employs soft, organic atmospheric depth. Backdrop blurs (16px to 24px) paired with tinted ambient glows (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.06)`) beneath cards impart a calm, floating tactility.
- **Hunter RPG Mode:** Uses high-contrast hard outlines and holographic projection. Cards feature `#00e5ff` edge glows (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`), accented with micro 1px cyan borders and clipped corners to simulate tactile physical heads-up displays.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a baseline roundedness token of `2` (0.5rem / 8px for standard components, 1rem for cards, 1.5rem for dialog sheets), adapting per archetype to convey distinct tactile qualities:

- **Universal Clean:** Standard 8px (`0.5rem`) radius for buttons, progress bars, and data inputs; 16px (`1rem`) radius for metric cards.
- **Women-Centric Wellness:** Elevated curvature. Inputs and action elements shift to soft 16px (`1rem`) radiuses, while primary cards feature 24px (`1.5rem`) silhouettes to evoke fluidity, biological naturalism, and calm.
- **Hunter RPG:** Precision-clipped or restrained 4px to 8px radiuses. Badges and high-rank quest monitors use chamfered/angled edge motifs (`polygon()` accents) evoking hard sci-fi UI geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Actions:** Full-width mobile touch target (min height: 48px). In Universal Clean, filled with Electric Cyan with black text; in Women-Centric, soft gradient from Rose Quartz to Blush Terracotta; in Hunter RPG, pure Obsidian with a high-intensity 1.5px Neon Cyan border and glowing hover resonance.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with tinted 1px perimeter border and monospaced supplementary text.

### Metric Cards & Containers
- Built on persistent surface tokens with minimum interior padding of `space-md`. 
- Header section reserves fixed space for biometric labels (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase) paired with right-aligned status indicators (pulsing dot for real-time telemetry).
- Data display clusters primary numbers (`label-numeric` or `headline-lg`) immediately beside trend deltas (e.g., `+4.2% HRV`).

### Status Badges & Rank Indicators
- **Universal Clean:** Subdued pill badges with solid tonal fill and colored dot markers.
- **Women-Centric Wellness:** Soft pastel pill badges denoting cycle stages (e.g., "Follicular • Day 8", "Deep Sleep Phase") with smooth color transitions.
- **Hunter RPG:** Angular shield badges (`label-badge`) denoting Hunter Rank ("RANK S", "DAILY QUEST COMPLETED", "BUFF ACTIVE") with gold, neon cyan, or warning crimson outlines.

### Bottom Navigation Dock
- Floating persistent dock anchor pinned 16px above the home indicator bar.
- Ultra-frosted glass backdrop (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.85)` + 20px blur) holding 4 to 5 core navigation icons.
- Active states:
  - Universal Clean: Crisp cyan indicator dot with illuminated vector glyph.
  - Women-Centric: Soft rose ambient pill backing the active glyph.
  - Hunter RPG: Angular HUD brackets surrounding the active tab accompanied by a holographic cyan accent beam.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background luminance matching the surface layer with inset 1px borders. Focused states trigger border color change to the active theme primary accompanied by a 2px outer ambient aura. Text inputs use `body-lg` to eliminate iOS auto-zoom on mobile viewports.
